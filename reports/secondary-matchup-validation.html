Why the saved games are excluded

  • Current active flags, depth roles and eventual participation do not prove historical pregame eligibility.
  • Prior-week statistics alone do not prove a complete input was available and frozen before kickoff.
  • The library does not retain reviewed full passing-play inventories for historical last-four evidence.
  • Missing fantasy scoring events and absent player rows remain unknown, not zero.

All 64 candidate games and their individual exclusions are recorded in reports/secondary-feasibility-2026.json. The audit was read-only. Recorded score fields were not treated as independently reviewed authoritative targets.

What was verified

A read-only paired evaluator now validates reviewed pregame rosters, complete input freezes, last-four passing sources and authoritative observed outcomes. It keeps calibration and evaluation periods separate, locks model sources, and uses the same 200-run production seed schedule for both models.

  • Both models match their production ensemble scoring summaries.
  • The passive every-play observer does not change model output or consume randomness.
  • Frozen possession-v1 output remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
  • Tests cover chronology, source identities, roster mismatches, missing fantasy events, passing/YAC denominators, and empty-sample safeguards.
  • Workspace type checks pass. All test fixtures are synthetic, not accuracy evidence.

A save-schema mismatch for Michael Wilson’s existing 1.5 priority tier was also fixed. A forecast save/reload regression confirms the tier, frozen inputs, summaries and older saves are preserved. Simulation weights were not changed.

Decision and next evidence

Keep the secondary method experimental. Preserve frozen forecasts and do not tune to a team winner or desired player score. Collect independently timestamped pregame snapshots under a registered cohort, then compare separate held-out games once final scoring, passing plays and complete fantasy ledgers are available.
