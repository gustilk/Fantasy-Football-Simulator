---
name: Manual high-scoring matchups
description: User-approved scoring environment adjustment for selected DraftKings matchups.
---

The user wants to check individual high-scoring games in the DraftKings tab so their player projections reflect a higher-scoring game environment. They chose “20% higher expected score” and approved changing the expected combined game score rather than giving every player an equal fantasy-point multiplier.

**Why:** Ranking high-scoring matchups alone did not meet their request; they want their manual matchup choices to affect new DraftKings projections.

**How to apply:** Treat the checkbox as a user-supplied, unvalidated scoring assumption, not verified evidence or a guaranteed result. Preserve complete outcomes and consistent offense/defense scoring, disclose any inability to reach the target, and freeze selections in forecasts. Changing a current checkbox must not alter an older saved forecast. Do not promise every player's projection rises by 20%.
