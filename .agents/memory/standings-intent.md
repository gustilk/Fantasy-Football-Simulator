name: Division standings and win pressure
description: User-selected manual win-pressure rule and point-in-time standings scope.
---

The user wants manually entered NFL standings grouped by division and chose “I mark the teams” for identifying teams that need a win.

**Why:** They want division-game strategy to account for win pressure, without an automatic standings-based estimate of playoff necessity.

**How to apply:** Keep the pressure decision under user control, team-specific and limited to division opponents. Treat it as an explicitly unvalidated strategy assumption, not an effort measurement or guaranteed scoring boost. Scope standings to records entering the selected season/week; preserve frozen forecasts rather than consulting newer standings during replay.
