name: Projection display intent
description: The user's distinction between player count and a fantasy-point cutoff.
---

The user wants projected lists to show 20 players; they do not require those players to project for 20 or more points.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified “20 players” versus “20+ points.”

**How to apply:** Treat 20 as the display count, not a scoring threshold. Preserve lower-point candidates in ranked projections and retain availability rules; do not invent players when fewer are available.
